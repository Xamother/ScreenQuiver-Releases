# Code of conduct

Keep discussions about ScreenQuiver respectful, useful and focused on the work.
People of any background or experience level are welcome to report problems,
suggest improvements and help with the documentation.

- Critique ideas and behaviour without personal attacks.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or impersonate other people.
- Do not publish someone else's private information, unwanted sexual content,
  spam or unrelated advertising.
- Respect different needs, including accessibility requirements, and accept
  reasonable moderation requests.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, comments and other project spaces
in this repository. Xamother maintains the project and may edit or remove
inappropriate content, restrict participation or block repeat offenders.

Use GitHub's **Report content** action on the relevant contribution to raise a
conduct concern through the reporting options GitHub provides. Avoid repeating
private information in a public report. Security vulnerabilities have a separate
reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
